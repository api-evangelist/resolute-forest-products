# Resolute Forest Products

Resolute Forest Products is a global leader in the forest products industry, producing market pulp, tissue, wood products, and newsprint. Formerly one of the largest pulp and paper companies in North America, the company operates manufacturing facilities across Canada and the United States. In 2023, Resolute was acquired by the Paper Excellence Group and now operates as a subsidiary of Domtar. The company supplies products to customers in more than 70 countries.

## About

Resolute Forest Products (now a Domtar/Paper Excellence Group company) operates across approximately 20 manufacturing facilities in Quebec, Ontario, and several U.S. states. The company produces:

- **Market Pulp** — Chemical wood pulp sold on the open market for paper and tissue manufacturing
- **Tissue Products** — Facial tissue, bath tissue, and paper towels
- **Wood Products** — Dimensional lumber, engineered wood, and wood pellets
- **Newsprint** — Lightweight paper for newspaper printing
